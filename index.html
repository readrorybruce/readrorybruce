About Rory

As an investigator of myth and a theorist of speculative divergence, my writing is concerned with the variance in outcomes of the human experience as our civilization continues its advancement.

Holding a bachelor’s degree from The Evergreen State College, I combine my academic experience with my background in music. Once trained as a cellist, I infuse my writing with musical methods, and base my craft as expression beyond formula.

A local of the Puget Sound area, I enjoy my time exploring PNW markets, wandering the woods, and reflecting at the shores of the Pacific.
